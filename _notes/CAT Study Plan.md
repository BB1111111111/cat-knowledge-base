# CAT Study Plan

## Principles
- Prioritise consistency over unrealistic daily targets.
- Practise all three sections throughout preparation.
- Track accuracy, time spent, question selection and error patterns.
- Analyse mocks deeply; taking a mock without analysis has limited value.

## Mock strategy
- Before: decide the purpose of the mock (baseline, strategy test, or exam simulation).
- During: note question selection and time allocation.
- After: classify every missed, guessed and skipped question.
- Follow up: practise the underlying concept and revisit the error after a few days.
